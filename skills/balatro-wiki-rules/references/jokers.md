# 150 Joker Ability Summaries

Use this collection-ordered adaptation of [Jokers](https://balatrowiki.org/w/Jokers) to find an object's main ability. For rarity, base cost, primary activation category, and individual article links, read [objects.md](objects.md). Before implementation, load the individual article for copying, growth, destruction, and generation exceptions.

Notation: C=Chips, M=Mult, X=multiply Mult. Round means an actual completed round. Full deck includes all surviving playing cards. Growth mutates the owned entity; the catalog's main activation category does not establish exact mutation timing.
