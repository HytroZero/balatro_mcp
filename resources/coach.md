# Balatro Gold Stake Coach

Win the current run's Ante 8 Boss with consistent damage, sustainable cash flow and useful growth. Continue at its actual stake and do not restart for a better opening. Outside Blue Deck, do not force a Pair engine. Use observed effects and values for PC 1.0.1o-FULL.

Follow `balatro://protocol` for execution, recovery and action explanations, and `balatro://guide` for state interpretation. Read each once; do not reload already available references. For a specific mechanic, use `balatro://objects/<name>` and its linked rule topic.

## Decision priorities

Survival comes first, then the revealed Boss, reliable growth and economy. Judge an upgrade by the next required blind and the showdown, not by the last easy clear. Check whether a critical effect expires or its carrying cost removes future choices.

Preserve interest when output is sufficient; spend below a savings threshold when it buys survival. Evaluate an Eternal purchase as a permanent commitment. For temporary or Rental support, compare its useful lifetime and carrying cost with the bridge it provides.

Prefer the engine the run actually supports. Compare hand levels, scoring cards, held value and Joker contributions; reinforce the weakest useful part rather than filling a prescribed five-slot layout.

## Blue Deck engine priority

On Blue Deck, rank engines: Pair (standard, or Blue Seal held for Mercury) > Two Pair > Flush > High Card / Four of a Kind > others. Spend Planets, Jokers and card upgrades along that order unless the observed deck already strongly supports a lower one. Do not build toward Straight: avoid Straight-conditioned Jokers (Crazy, Devious, Runner, The Order, Shortcut, Superposition) and especially never buy an Eternal one, because it permanently occupies a slot in an engine this deck should not run.

## Progress through the run

**Antes 1–2:** take flexible immediate scoring and establish income; on Blue Deck keep to its engine priority and treat Straight support as temporary at most. Temporary support can buy access to more shops; speculative growth needs a credible bridge.

**Antes 3–5:** develop repeatable damage and growth together. Use safe rounds to build hand levels, scaling or value generation. Check the next Ante early enough to improve before its Boss.

**Antes 6–8:** spend for the remaining route, replace obsolete support when legal, and verify the revealed restrictions against conservative damage. Preserve a real countermeasure when the engine depends on one.

## Compare alternatives

- Weigh immediate score against draw improvement and the cost of another hand/discard. Do not chase an ideal draw when a reliable finish is already available.
- Compare scoring a valuable card with leaving it held. A play can also act as a discard: fill it to five cards with dead cards that do not score, so they leave the hand without spending a discard. Use this when discards are scarce, after checking that the extra cards do not change the poker hand or give up held rewards.
- Check both scoring-card and held-card order when their effects interact. A search timeout can omit a better arrangement; price your own legal line.
- Usually play blinds for income, shops and growth. Skip only for a concrete benefit that outweighs those losses.
- Stop spending when an offer has less route value than the cash retained.
